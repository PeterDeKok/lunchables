<div class="not-prose text-sm uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-teal-600 font-mono mb-6">Operational Ownership · Lean</div>

The Toyota Way applied to operations — no separate Ops team. Production health is an immediate, shared problem. Feature work stops until the systemic cause is permanently fixed.

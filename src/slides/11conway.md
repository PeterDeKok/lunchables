<div class="not-prose text-sm uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-teal-600 font-mono mb-6">Industry Frameworks · Conway's Law</div>

<div class="not-prose bg-gray-900 rounded-2xl px-10 py-8 mb-6">
  <div class="text-4xl italic text-gray-200 leading-snug">"Organizations produce designs that mirror their communication structures."</div>
  <div class="text-base text-gray-500 mt-5">— Melvin Conway, 1967</div>
</div>

<div class="not-prose grid grid-cols-2 gap-5">
  <div class="bg-gray-900 rounded-2xl p-8 flex flex-col gap-3">
    <div class="text-sm uppercase tracking-widest text-gray-500">Team Topologies (Skelton & Pais, 2019)</div>
    <div class="text-2xl leading-relaxed">Best teams are <span class="underline decoration-wavy decoration-teal-300 underline-offset-8">stream-aligned</span> — end-to-end delivery, no handoffs between teams</div>
  </div>
  <div class="bg-gray-900 rounded-2xl p-8 flex flex-col gap-3">
    <div class="text-sm uppercase tracking-widest text-gray-500">The implication</div>
    <div class="text-2xl leading-relaxed">Architecture and ownership must be <strong class="text-gray-100">symmetrical</strong> — four teams required to release one feature will be felt in every delay</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="not-prose mt-6 text-base text-gray-600 leading-relaxed"><span class="text-gray-500">Stream-aligned</span> — organized around a continuous flow of work for a single business domain or user journey, not a technical layer. The team owns design, build, test, deploy, and operate for that domain without needing to hand off to a frontend, backend, or ops team for routine work.</div>

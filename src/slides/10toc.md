<div class="not-prose text-sm uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-teal-600 font-mono mb-6">Industry Frameworks · Theory of Constraints</div>

_Eliyahu Goldratt_, "The Goal" (1984) — any system is limited by its single narrowest bottleneck.

Picture a factory where every machine runs at half capacity _except one_. <br />Every upstream improvement just creates more backlog at that machine.

<div class="not-prose flex flex-col gap-5 mt-8">
  <div class="flex items-baseline gap-6">
    <div class="text-5xl font-black text-teal-700 w-14 shrink-0">1</div>
    <div class="text-2xl">Find the <span class="underline decoration-wavy decoration-teal-300 underline-offset-8">constraint</span></div>
  </div>
  <div class="flex items-baseline gap-6">
    <div class="text-5xl font-black text-teal-700 w-14 shrink-0">2</div>
    <div class="text-2xl">Exploit it fully before expanding capacity elsewhere</div>
  </div>
  <div class="flex items-baseline gap-6">
    <div class="text-5xl font-black text-teal-700 w-14 shrink-0">3</div>
    <div class="text-2xl">Elevate — remove the constraint once it is fully exploited</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="not-prose mt-8 text-2xl text-gray-400">Ownership models create or remove bottlenecks. The approval queue is often where the constraint lives.</div>

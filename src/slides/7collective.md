<div class="not-prose text-sm uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-teal-600 font-mono mb-6">Code Ownership · Collective Ownership</div>

Popularized by _Extreme Programming_ (Kent Beck) — any team member can change any part of the codebase.

<div class="not-prose grid grid-cols-2 gap-16 mt-8">
  <div>
    <div class="text-sm uppercase tracking-widest text-gray-500 border-b border-gray-800 pb-3 mb-6">Strengths</div>
    <div class="text-3xl mb-5">Zero single points of failure</div>
    <div class="text-3xl mb-5">High team autonomy</div>
    <div class="text-3xl mb-5">Fast feature development</div>
  </div>
  <div>
    <div class="text-sm uppercase tracking-widest text-gray-500 border-b border-gray-800 pb-3 mb-6">Trade-offs</div>

    <div class="text-3xl mb-5">Requires very mature CI/CD</div>
  </div>
</div>

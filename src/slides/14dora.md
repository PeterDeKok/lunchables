_DevOps Research and Assessment_ — 36,000+ professionals surveyed (2013–2023).

The research asked a specific question: does requiring more human sign-offs make releases safer?

<div class="not-prose grid grid-cols-2 gap-5 mt-6">
  <div class="flex flex-col gap-5">

      <div class="text-sm uppercase tracking-widest text-gray-500">Finding 1</div>
      <div class="text-2xl leading-relaxed">More centralized approvals → slower lead time, less frequent deploys</div>
    </div>
    <div class="bg-gray-900 rounded-2xl p-7 border border-teal-900 flex flex-col gap-3">
      <div class="text-sm uppercase tracking-widest text-teal-600">Finding 2 — counterintuitive</div>
      <div class="text-2xl leading-relaxed">More centralized approvals → <strong class="text-gray-100">no measurable reduction</strong> in change failure rate</div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col gap-5">
    <div class="bg-gray-900 rounded-2xl p-7 flex flex-col gap-3">
      <div class="text-sm uppercase tracking-widest text-gray-500">What does work</div>

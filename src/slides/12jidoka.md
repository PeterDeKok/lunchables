<div class="not-prose text-sm uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-teal-600 font-mono mb-6">Industry Frameworks · Jidoka</div>

Traditional manufacturing placed a quality inspector at the _end_ of the line. <br />The Toyota Production System abolished this role entirely.

_Jidoka_ (autonomation) — machines and people both have the authority to detect a defect and stop production immediately.
